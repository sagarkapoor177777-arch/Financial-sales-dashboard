# Financial-sales-dashboard
## Findings

- The dashboard provides an overview of Sales, Profit, and Units Sold.
- Paseo is the highest-selling product based on the Sales by Product analysis.
- The United States of America has the highest sales among the countries shown.
- Government has the highest profit among the segments shown.
- Sales show fluctuations over time with noticeable peaks during certain periods.

## Recommendations

- Focus on high-performing products such as Paseo to maximize sales opportunities.
- Analyze the United States market to identify opportunities for further growth.
- Study the Government segment to understand the factors contributing to higher profitability.
- Monitor sales trends and plan inventory and promotional activities around high-performing periods.
